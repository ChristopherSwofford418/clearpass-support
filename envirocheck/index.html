Scores and location

EnviroCheck combines available air, water, UV, pollen, and related indicators into an informational local score. Location is optional: you can search by city or ZIP code. Scores are not medical advice, emergency alerts, or a substitute for public-health guidance.

Subscriptions and restore purchases

EnviroCheck Pro subscriptions are billed by Apple and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage subscriptions in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

To restore: open the Pro screen, tap Restore Purchases, and ensure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for purchase.

Troubleshooting

Update EnviroCheck, confirm an internet connection, then close and reopen the app. If a score is unavailable, try a nearby city or ZIP code and retry later.
