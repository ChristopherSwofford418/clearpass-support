Email support

Restore a Pro purchase

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Open the Pro or subscription section.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.
  4. Confirm that the device uses the Apple Account that made the purchase.

If the purchase still does not appear, confirm App Store connectivity and try again.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions, or tap the subscription-management action in NaijaMacros. Apple controls billing, cancellation, eligibility, and refunds.

Food result looks wrong

Nutrient values are estimates. Review the food match, serving size, and quantity before saving. For packaged products, use the product label as the source of truth. You can create a custom food when the library does not match.

Barcode or label scan

Allow camera access when prompted, provide good lighting, and keep the barcode or label steady. Label text recognition runs on device. If scanning fails, enter the food manually.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. Review permissions in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices. NaijaMacros can function without Apple Health access.

Delete or export local data

Open Profile → Privacy & Data. Export creates a file you can save or share. Delete Local Data permanently removes the app’s local profile, logs, recipes, meals, goals, and related history from that installation. Apple purchase history and Apple Health records are controlled separately by Apple.

Notifications

Enable reminders in Profile and allow notifications when prompted. iOS may delay or suppress notifications. Do not rely on reminders for urgent medical needs.

What to include in an email

Describe the screen, what you expected, and what happened. You may include a screenshot if it contains no information you do not want to share. Never send passwords or payment-card details.
