NaijaMacros

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · NaijaMacros was previously named NaijaFuel.

Release summary: the current App Store release is local-first. It does not enable a cloud account or cloud synchronization. Your food logs, goals, recipes, saved meals, and optional weight entries remain on your device unless you choose a separate Apple Health, notification, App Store purchase, export, or support action.

Information stored on your device

NaijaMacros can store a profile, nutrition goals, food logs, custom foods, recipes, saved meals, optional weight entries, onboarding status, settings, and subscription status locally on your device. These records support the app features you use.

On-device food and image processing

Meal-description matching and protein suggestions use the app’s built-in Nigerian food library in this release. Nutrition-label text recognition runs on your device. The app does not upload your chosen label image in the current release. Barcode results may be looked up from a public food database; you can always review or enter values manually before saving.

Camera and photo access

The camera is requested only when you choose a barcode or nutrition-label scan. Photo-library access is requested only when you choose a photo from your library. You can decline either permission and use manual entry.

Apple Health

If you explicitly enable Apple Health, NaijaMacros requests only the health data types shown in Apple’s authorization sheet. Health data is not used for advertising or sold. You can change Apple Health permissions in iOS Settings.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. You can disable them in the app or in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases and renewals are handled by Apple. The app receives product and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features. ClearPass Technologies does not receive your full payment-card details.

Analytics

Analytics is off by default. No production analytics provider is configured in the current App Store release. The app does not sell personal information and does not use nutrition, health, photo, or account content for targeted advertising.

Export and deletion

You can export local data from Profile. You can permanently delete local app data from Profile; uninstalling the app also removes its local container, subject to normal iOS backup behavior. Apple purchase history and Apple Health records are controlled through Apple.

Retention

Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app. Data handled by Apple is subject to Apple’s policies. A support email is retained only as needed to answer and document the request.

Children

NaijaMacros is a general-wellness nutrition tool and is not designed to diagnose, treat, or manage a child’s medical condition. A parent, guardian, or qualified healthcare professional should supervise any use involving a minor.

Security

Data on your device receives the protections provided by iOS and your device security settings. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Questions or privacy requests: info@clearpasstechnologies.com.
