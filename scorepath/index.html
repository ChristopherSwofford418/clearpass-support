Plans and progress

ScorePath helps you structure personal goals into practical next steps. If a plan does not load, confirm an internet connection, close and reopen the app, and try again.

Subscriptions and restore purchases

ScorePath subscriptions are billed by Apple and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

To restore a subscription, open ScorePath’s subscription screen, tap Restore Purchases, and make sure you are using the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Troubleshooting

Install the latest ScorePath update, confirm a network connection, and restart the app. If the issue continues, email support with the app version shown in your device settings.
